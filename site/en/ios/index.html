Compatibility

Does it work with my bike?

You need a motorcycle with a TFT dash that shows a QR code to connect a phone (MotoPlay / EasyConnect). The app is tested daily on a Zontes 125X and has already worked with other dashes, such as the JCDZ34. If yours shows that QR, give it a try: the app tells you whether it is compatible.

Getting started

Ready in five minutes

  1. Download it

    Install RideScreen from the App Store and open the app. Accept the Bluetooth, local network and camera permissions.

  2. Pair the dash

    On Home tap More → Pair and scan the dash's QR. Join its Wi‑Fi once.

  3. Connect

    Tap Connect. The picture appears on the dash, and the app records the ride meanwhile. After that it connects by itself.

  4. Optional: the key

    In the PKE settings, scan the bike's information QR. If the box does not answer, add the official app's Bluetooth password.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost?

The app is free. With it you can pair, connect and see the dash with speed and time. The full unlock (designs, map, OBD, key, rides, routes, Siri…) is a single payment, no subscription, and works on all your devices with the same Apple ID.

Can I use Waze or Google Maps on the dash?

No. On iPhone the app can only draw its own picture with the phone locked, and that is done with the Apple map. You can paste a destination from Google Maps and it navigates it.

The keyless key does not respond

If the box answers but does not obey, type in the PKE settings the 6-character Bluetooth password you set when you bound the bike in the official app. Leave it empty if it already works.

What data does it collect?

Your rides and settings stay on your iPhone. There are no accounts or ads. Sending anonymous data is optional and never includes your location. It is all in the privacy policy.

Something does not work

Settings → Anonymous data and problems → Report a problem, or write to hola@ridescreen.app with your bike and what happened.
