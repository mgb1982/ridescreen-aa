# Google Play listing · English

**Title (21/30):** RideScreen: Moto Dash

**Short description (76/80):** Wireless Android Auto for compatible Carbit/EasyConnect motorcycle displays.

**Full description (under 4,000 characters):**

Bring Android Auto to your motorcycle's dashboard from your Android phone, over Wi‑Fi. RideScreen is an independent fork of OpenCfMoto for Carbit/EasyConnect dashboards (the ones that show a QR code to pair a phone through MotoPlay/EasyConnect). It is ridden daily on a Zontes 125X and riders have confirmed it on the Zontes 125V, 125M, 368 (G/K/E), 703RR, T350X, 703F and 703T. Compatibility depends on the motorcycle and its dashboard.

**On the dash**

- Google Maps, Waze, Spotify and the Assistant on your bike's screen.
- Auto‑connect when you start the bike, with the phone in your pocket.
- Live screen fit and voice assistant.

**Around the ride**

- Parking spot with photo and walking directions.
- Cheapest petrol nearby (Spain), maintenance log and real fuel consumption.
- Ride log coloured by speed, heat map, records, trophies and your year in review.
- Ride video with map and speed overlay, and live location sharing.
- Crash detection (experimental, opt‑in): alarm on phone and watch, then an SMS with your location to up to three contacts. It is not a certified safety device.
- Wear OS app: next turn, compass to your parked bike, D‑pad for Android Auto and the crash alarm.

**Keyless box (PKE)**

On the Zontes 125X, RideScreen can read the keyless box's battery voltage and lock state over Bluetooth and, if you set it up, lock or unlock the bike and release the seat or fuel cap from the app or a home‑screen widget. Setup needs your bike's PKE code and the McuID from its information QR; the McuID stays on the phone and is excluded from backups. Unlock on Connect is optional, off by default, and only acts when you tap Connect, never on automatic connection. Seat and fuel cap ask for confirmation. Check the bike after each command. Other bikes and keyless boxes have not been tested.

RideScreen is free, open‑source software under AGPL‑3.0‑or‑later, with no ads. It is **not an official Zontes or CFMoto app** and is not affiliated with or endorsed by either company. Android Auto is a trademark of Google LLC.
